Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. What we collect

  • TikTok: when you connect your account through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives your basic profile (open_id, display name, avatar) and OAuth access and refresh tokens for the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload.
  • YouTube: when you connect a channel through Google OAuth, the App receives OAuth tokens for the scope youtube.upload.

The App does not collect data from viewers, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not access your followers, messages or any content other than the videos it uploads.

2. How we use it

Tokens are used only to upload videos that you approved to your own accounts and to check the upload status.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted in a database on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared, or sent to anyone other than TikTok and Google when calling their APIs.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account remains connected. You can delete them at any time by revoking the App's access in your TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by requesting deletion at the contact below; the stored tokens are then removed.

5. Third parties

Your use of TikTok and YouTube is also governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy. The App uses YouTube API Services.
